## Q1. Product by product: what wallet-discovery features exist, which metrics/filters/windows, which labels, and how are the labels defined?

### Takeaway
Only five products publish any label definition at all (GMGN, Solana Tracker, Cielo, Nansen, and the data vendors Codex/Mobula), and even those are one-line descriptions with almost no thresholds; every mass-market terminal (Axiom, Photon, BullX, Trojan, Bloom, Padre/Terminal, Maestro, Nova, BONKbot) exposes wallet *tracking* (paste an address, get alerts) but little or no wallet *discovery*, and where a leaderboard exists it is ranked by the leader's own realized PnL over 1/7/30/90-day windows. The "smart money" label is, across the field, a circular definition ("wallet who often earns money") applied to the leader's own PnL.

## Q2. Which products rank by what a COPIER would have made (follower-realisable return) versus the leader's own PnL? Does anyone model lag or fees?

### Takeaway
Every mass-market leaderboard (Kolscan, Solana Tracker, Cielo, Nansen, GMGN, Fomo) ranks on the leader's own realized PnL; only two small 2026 entrants claim a follower-side metric — KOL Brain (published formula: ~3 s latency, ~5 % slippage) and Wallet Master ("only trades you could have copied yourself … fees and tips deducted", method undisclosed) — and one data vendor (Solana Tracker) lists "Copy-Trade Performance" as coming soon. Lag is modelled only at execution time (Bloom's Max Slot Diff) or as a qualitative warning (Trojan: snipers land "several blocks behind").

### Cited Findings
- Kolscan ranks by SOL gain per Daily/Weekly/Monthly tab with no follower metric — [kolscan.io/leaderboard](https://kolscan.io/leaderboard); third parties say it "sorts by realized PnL by default" — [Kalkine](https://kalkinemedia.com/education/guides/kolscan-how-solanas-smart-money-gets-ranked) (T, 2026-07-16)
- Solana Tracker's top-traders default sort is `realized`; its own docs say the filters "do not predict future returns" — [leaderboards guide](https://docs.solanatracker.io/guides/pnl-v2/leaderboards.md); "Copy-Trade Performance" is listed under "Coming soon" — [solanatracker.io/leaderboard](https://www.solanatracker.io/leaderboard)
- Cielo Wallet Discovery ranks by "realized PnL" over 1/7/30 days — [Cielo Wallet Discovery](https://docs.cielo.finance/wallet-tracking/discovery/wallet-discovery.md)
- Nansen Smart Trader = "Top-ranked wallets by PnL in past 30 days" — [Nansen Labels 101](https://academy.nansen.ai/articles/2149924-labels-and-watchlists-101)
- GMGN Discover columns are the wallet's own 1D/7D/30D PnL and 7D win rate — [GMGN DISCOVER](https://docs.gmgn.ai/index/discover.md)
- **KOL Brain "Copyability"** = "a 0–100 estimate of how much of a wallet's edge survives being followed with about 3 seconds of latency and about 5% slippage"; components: entry market cap 20 %, profit per position 20 %, hold time vs latency 18 %, execution pace 14 %, edge type 16 %, shrunk win rate vs population 12 %; a "fidelity gate" multiplies quality by 0.25 + 0.75·f where f is the worse of hold-time and pace scores; "Many top wallets hold for seconds: a low score is the honest result, not a criticism"; "Hard to copy" flag when copyability < 35 and track score ≥ 65; unrealized PnL "is never used"; results are "model outputs, not guarantees of what a follower would earn" — [KOL Brain methodology](https://kolbrain.fun/methodology)

- Cielo: sniper wallets (avg hold < 2 min) — "Manual copying is unlikely to match the results"; flippers "can multiply fees when copied" — [Cielo Finding Good Wallets](https://docs.cielo.finance/guides/copy-trading/finding-good-wallets.md)

- (N, 2026-10-20) An independent test: entering two minutes after large wallet clusters bought "lost 41% per token on average, with a median decline of 95%" across 93 tokens; a single-wallet copy backtest "lost 20% per trade"; following clusters with positive records lost 11 %/token; a small set of "tightly connected groups" gained ~10 % per token across 16 tokens; the work "did not identify a monetizable trading edge" — [TokenPost](https://www.tokenpost.com/news/investing/35409) (the 10-24 internal doc attributes this study to White Whale Labs via ChainCatcher; TokenPost does not name the author)

- (T) MadeOnSol's 400-wallet filter does not claim copy profitability and warns "following bots makes you their exit liquidity" — [MadeOnSol methodology](https://madeonsol.com/blog/solana-alpha-wallets-methodology-952k-dataset)

### Inferences
- KOL Brain is the only public product with a formula that resembles Krypto Copy's follower-realisable score; its 3 s / 5 % assumptions are fixed constants, not measured per session, and its "hold time vs latency" term is a proxy for reachability rather than a repriced fill. It also keys off *leader* profit per position, so it still inherits leader-PnL selection bias.
- Wallet Master's claim is the closest to a true repricing ("trades you could have copied yourself") but is unverifiable; its $250/mo API would be the only external benchmark against which Krypto Copy's shrunk follower median could be compared.
- Solana Tracker adding "Copy-Trade Performance" means the field's largest PnL data vendor will soon publish a follower metric; its definition should be checked when it ships, because if it uses the leader's next print rather than a lagged follower fill it will reproduce the leader-PnL bias.

### Gaps
- No product publishes a lag curve, a per-wallet "entry premium" (follower fill vs leader fill), or a dead-coin treatment.
- Wallet Master's follower-realisable method (lag assumed, cost model) is members-only and was not fetched.
- The TokenPost study's primary source (methodology, wallet list, cluster definition) was not retrieved.

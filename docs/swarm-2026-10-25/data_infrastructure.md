# Data sources and infrastructure for whole-market Solana memecoin wallet scoring (as of 2026-10-25)

Scope: what a desktop copy-trading app can use to see every pump.fun curve trade, PumpSwap and Raydium swap well enough to score tens of thousands of wallets a week on follower-realisable return, on a small budget. Prices were read from vendor pages on 2026-10-25 unless marked otherwise. Anything measured inside the Krypto Copy repo is labelled **repo-measured** and cited to the file. Web search ran out of budget before the last market-scale pass; the gaps say where.

Conventions: "credits" are vendor units; Helius overage is about $5 per million credits (inferred below). Slot time is 211.6 ms (repo-measured, Alpenglow), so one day is about 408k slots and one week about 2.86M.

## Q1. Yellowstone gRPC / Geyser providers: tiers, filters, bandwidth, replay

### Takeaway
Whole-market streaming is sold three ways in 2026: metered by bandwidth (Helius LaserStream 2 credits/0.1 MB on Business $499+; QuickNode 10 credits/0.1 MB on Scale $499+; Triton $0.08/GB pay-as-you-go; Alchemy $75/TB), flat-rate with connection caps (Shyft Build $199 unlimited bandwidth; Corvus $160; Solana Vibe Station $150; Chainstack add-on $49 on a $49 Growth plan), or free (publicnode's Yellowstone endpoint with an allnodes token, unverified coverage). A single pump.fun + PumpSwap program filter runs about 10 GB/h on gRPC (one measured capture), which makes metered plans the most expensive route and makes a residential desktop a poor terminal for the full market; replay windows range from ~150 slots (Shyft) to 48 h (Helius, Alchemy).

### Inferences
- Whole-market monthly bandwidth ≈ 10 GB/h × 720 h ≈ 7.2 TB (one capture; the repo's 1.26 + ~7 GB/h estimate agrees at ~6 TB). Priced: Helius 20,000 credits/GB → ~144M credits ≈ Business's 100M plus ~$220 overage (~$720 total) or Professional + 10 TB add-on ($1,749); Triton ≈ $580; Alchemy ≈ $540; QuickNode 100,000 credits/GB → ~720M of Scale's 950M credits ($499); Shyft/Corvus/SVS flat at $150–$199; Chainstack $98 if the add-on really is unmetered (not stated).
- Curve-only (1.26 GB/h ≈ 0.9 TB/month): Triton ≈ $73, Alchemy ≈ $68, Helius ≈ 18M credits (above Developer's 10M even before any RPC use, so Business $499).
- To a desktop: 10 GB/h is ~22 Mbps sustained and ~240 GB/day; curve-only is ~2.8 Mbps and ~30 GB/day. A whole-market stream wants a VPS, not a home PC, and only runs while the machine is on; curve-only is feasible on a desktop that is on all day.
- A desktop client can hold one stream for both programs on any provider above (one transaction filter with two program ids; Shyft allows 150k addresses in a filter and 10 filters per subscribe). Replay after a hiccup is provider-specific: Shyft ~150 slots (~32 s) is too short for a laptop sleep; QuickNode 3,000 slots (~10.6 min at 211.6 ms); Helius/Alchemy 48 h; Yardswitch 24 h. Beyond the window, gaps must be backfilled by RPC (see Q5).

### Gaps
- No ERPC pricing was found. InstantNodes pricing is from a third-party profile only; its own pricing page redirected to an unrelated domain on 2026-10-25.
- Chainstack does not state whether gRPC bandwidth is metered, nor its replay window.
- Fumarole (Triton's persistent/replayable stream) semantics were not retrievable; Triton's docs mention it only by name.
- No provider publishes a measured end-to-end latency for a pump.fun program filter; the repo measured Helius Parsed Streams at a median 119 ms ahead of `logsSubscribe` (Q2), not gRPC.
- The 10 GB/h figure is one developer's two-minute capture (Aug 2026); no second measurement was found.

## Q2. Helius in 2026: Parsed Streams, webhooks, getTransactionsForAddress, credit costs, what 1M credits buys

### Takeaway
Helius is the cheapest per-wallet backfill and per-trip pricing source (10 credits per 100 full transactions, unlimited mainnet history, slot and blockTime filters, 1,000 per call) and the best free live source for followed wallets (Parsed Streams at 1 credit per event on Free, program filters allowed). It is the wrong tool for whole-market ingest: at ~25M pump/PumpSwap events a day, Parsed Streams would burn the Free month's 1M credits in about an hour and Professional's 200M in eight days, and metered LaserStream needs Business plus overage.

### Cited Findings
- Credit schedule: standard RPC 1; `getProgramAccounts` 10 (V2 1); `getBlock` 1; `getTransactionsForAddress` 10 per 100 full transactions (10 minimum; signatures-only 10 flat; failed responses free); `getTransfersByAddress` 10 (Developer+); Enhanced Transactions (legacy, maintenance mode) 100; Parsed Events 10 per request; DAS 10; webhooks 1 per event, 100 per management call; Parsed Streams 1 per delivered event; LaserStream 2 per 0.1 MB; Wallet API 100 per request; Sender 0 — [Helius credits](https://www.helius.dev/docs/billing/credits)
- Plans: Free $0 1M credits 10 RPC req/s; Developer $49 10M 50 req/s; Business $499 100M 200 req/s; Professional $999 200M 500 req/s; DAS/Enhanced/Wallet API 2/10/50/100 req/s; autoscaling on Business+; WaaS FAQ prices 200 credits at $0.001 (implies ~$5 per million) — [Helius plans](https://www.helius.dev/docs/billing/plans); [Helius rate limits](https://www.helius.dev/docs/billing/rate-limits)
- Rate-limit table: Parsed Streams 5/10/50/50 concurrent connections by plan, 25 subscriptions per connection, counted per project across keys; webhooks 5 on Free and 50 on paid, 100k addresses per webhook; `getTransactionsForAddress` cannot be batched (one address per request); `getTransaction` batch 100 — [Helius rate limits](https://www.helius.dev/docs/billing/rate-limits)
- Parsed Streams: "generally available on all plans, including Free"; filters `programs` (up to 10 per filter), `instructionNames` (up to 50), `accounts.include` (up to 100), `accounts.roles` (up to 20), `includeCpi`, `includeFailed`; "You can filter by any program address"; decodes 3,600+ programs including Pump.fun and PumpSwap, Raydium CLMM/CPMM, Jupiter v6, Meteora; delivered at confirmed; endpoint `wss://beta.helius-rpc.com`; outbound buffer 2,048 notifications per connection (connection closed if full); 10 client messages/s, burst 20 — [Helius Parsed Streams](https://www.helius.dev/docs/parsed-streams); [Parsed Streams reference](https://www.helius.dev/docs/api-reference/parsed-streams/overview.md)
- **Repo-measured (2026-10-24):** Parsed Streams on a free key, 20 busiest Scout wallets, 3 minutes: 5,630 transactions vs `logsSubscribe`'s 5,626 (none missing), median 119 ms earlier (p10 197, p90 56); payload carries pump's decoded TradeEvent but no pre/post balances, so sells still need a read-back (median 107 ms); Free is 5 connections × 25 subscriptions — [docs/engineering-2026-10-24.md #9](C:\Users\Krypt\Desktop\Krypto Copy\docs\engineering-2026-10-24.md)
- `getTransactionsForAddress`: up to 1,000 per call (default 1,000), `paginationToken` "slot:position"; filters `slot` (gte/gt/lte/lt), `blockTime`, `signature`, `status`, `tokenAccounts` (none/balanceChanged/all), `tokenTransfer` (mint, direction, counterparty, amount); mainnet retention unlimited; commitment `confirmed` or `finalized`; no program or type filter; free-plan access and rate limit not stated — [Helius getTransactionsForAddress](https://www.helius.dev/docs/rpc/gettransactionsforaddress)
- **Repo-measured (2026-10-24):** chain-priced replay of 30 trips: one `getTransactionsForAddress` on the coin with `filters.slot.gte` and `sortOrder: asc` returns up to 100 trades, a second call only when the exit lies past the page; 32 calls, 320 credits, 2.75 s for 30/30 trips; scan pages of 100 cost 10 credits and return in 195–300 ms with 74–96 trades; a full 60-coin scan ≤ ~1,800 credits — [docs/engineering-2026-10-24.md #10–#11](C:\Users\Krypt\Desktop\Krypto Copy\docs\engineering-2026-10-24.md); [electron/engine/chainPrice.ts](C:\Users\Krypt\Desktop\Krypto Copy\electron\engine\chainPrice.ts)
- **Repo-measured (2026-10-24):** a plain-Node prototype priced 4,320 legs in 14 min (~5 legs/s) at ~10 credits per call — [docs/copy-candidates-2026-10-24.md](C:\Users\Krypt\Desktop\Krypto Copy\docs\copy-candidates-2026-10-24.md)
- Webhooks: raw (all transactions for monitored addresses, no type filter, lower latency), enhanced (parsed, type-filtered), Discord; 1 credit per event charged whether or not the endpoint succeeds; retries can duplicate; a Free plan's webhooks are auto-disabled on a 24-hour evaluation window — [Helius webhooks](https://www.helius.dev/docs/webhooks)
- Wallet API `funded-by`: traces the first incoming SOL transfer; returns funder address, funder name/type if a known entity, signature, timestamp and slot; "requires a paid plan"; Free returns 403; 404 if never funded — [Helius funded-by](https://www.helius.dev/docs/wallet-api/funded-by)

### Inferences
- What 1M Free credits buy, by use: (i) followed-wallet live feed: 1 credit per delivered transaction, so 20 busy wallets at ~1,900 tx/min (repo sample) would exhaust the month in ~9 hours; realistic for 5–10 moderate wallets. (ii) Per-wallet backfill: a week of a 300-trade wallet is ≤ 3 full pages of 100 = 30 credits, or one 1,000-row page = 100 credits; pricing 30 trips costs ~320 credits (repo); so ≈ 350 credits per fully follower-scored wallet → ~2,800 wallets a month on the whole Free allowance, ~650 a week; the repo's 100k backtest allowance is ~300 replays. (iii) Whole-market via Parsed Streams: 24.8M events/day (Q4) → 1M credits ≈ 58 minutes; Developer 10M ≈ 10 h; Business 100M ≈ 4 days; Professional 200M ≈ 8 days. Curve-only (2.4–5.8M/day) stretches Free to 4–10 h.
- Developer ($49, 10M): ~28k fully scored wallets a month (≈6.5k a week) if every wallet's trips are priced from scratch; more if coin-page answers are cached across wallets (busy coins repeat), which `chain-prices.json` already does.
- A program-id webhook would deliver the whole market to a backend at 1 credit per transaction (same economics as Parsed Streams, ~750M credits/month) and requires a public HTTPS endpoint, so it buys nothing over a flat-rate gRPC stream.
- Helius keeps full mainnet history for `getTransactionsForAddress`, so weeks-deep backfill per wallet or per coin is possible at 0.1 credit per transaction; no other provider priced below that per transaction except bulk Parquet (Q5).

### Gaps
- Whether `getTransactionsForAddress` is served on the Free plan is not in the docs; the repo's live runs used the user's key, whose plan is not stated in the docs read.
- Helius does not publish indexing latency for `getTransactionsForAddress` (how soon after confirmation a trade is queryable), which matters for near-live scoring.
- The webhooks FAQ page (max addresses/program ids as monitored addresses) returned 404 on 2026-10-25; the 100k-addresses-per-webhook figure comes from the rate-limits page only.
- The Parsed Events API (10 credits/request) was not evaluated for history reads.

## Q3. Indexed and historical APIs: per-wallet and per-token trades, slot/time, cost, freshness, terms

### Takeaway
Only four indexed products expose per-wallet trade history with a public price: Solana Tracker (€50/mo for 200k calls, 1 call per credit, ToS allows building products but forbids raw-data redistribution and "competing products"), Moralis (`getSwapsByWalletAddress` 50 CU; $149/mo for 2M CU, no free self-serve), Solscan Pro (100 CU per call, Lite $49/mo for 20M CU but DeFi activities only 6 months deep and `funded_by` excluded), and Birdeye (trader endpoints ~15 CU; Starter $99/mo 3M CU). For the whole-market problem, Dune's `dex_solana.trades` (block_slot, trader_id, tx_id, ~3-hour refresh; Analyst $75/mo) and Bitquery (real-time only on self-serve; Personal $49/mo 100k points, 25k records per request) let one query score every wallet at once, which no per-wallet API can. Flipside, Allium, Goldsky and SolanaFM yielded no usable 2026 Solana pricing.

## Q5. Slot-accurate pricing at scale: alternatives to per-token getTransactionsForAddress

### Takeaway
If the app ingests every trade, slot-accurate pricing is free: each pump TradeEvent carries virtual reserves and each PumpSwap swap its executed amounts, so "price after the last trade at slot+9" is a lookup in the app's own table (the repo already prices this way from Helius pages at ~5 credits per leg). Without a full stream, the per-coin Helius page remains the cheapest exact method (0.1 credit per trade read; ~320 credits per 30-trip wallet). Block-by-block `getBlock` is 1 credit per slot on Helius but ~408k calls and hundreds of GB a day; Geyser replay only reaches back 150 slots to 48 h; Old Faithful is free but a multi-hundred-TB self-hosted archive; a $200-per-month-of-data Parquet archive is the cheapest whole-market historical backfill for backtests.

### Cited Findings
- Helius `getBlock` costs 1 credit; `getTransactionsForAddress` 10 per 100 full transactions with `slot` and `blockTime` range filters and unlimited mainnet history — [Helius credits](https://www.helius.dev/docs/billing/credits); [Helius getTransactionsForAddress](https://www.helius.dev/docs/rpc/gettransactionsforaddress)

### Inferences

- The soak's ~287 trades/s on a single development machine with ~35 MB RSS shows the compute side of whole-market ingest is trivial; the constraints are bandwidth (~10 GB/h), uptime and storage (~175M rows/week), which is why the pattern is a small always-on box plus a thin client.
- The indexer's "checkpoint never advances beyond what is written" plus RPC range backfill is exactly the replay-safe dedupe the repo's plan calls for (§5 item 4), and it works with any provider's `fromSlot` window because gaps beyond the window are filled by RPC.

## Q9. Proposed architectures: free, ~$50/mo, ~$500/mo

### Takeaway
Free keeps today's design (chain feed while the app runs, Helius Free for replay) and fully scores roughly 500–700 wallets a week with survivorship bias from partial live coverage. About $50 buys either Helius Developer (desktop-only, ~6k fully priced wallets a week, still partial market coverage) or a Dune Analyst seat ($75) that scores every active wallet weekly in SQL with a 3-hour lag, if `dex_solana.trades` covers the curve. About $500 buys the real answer: a flat-rate Yellowstone stream (Shyft $199 or Corvus $160) into a small VPS indexer (shaurya35/Carbon pattern) plus Helius Developer for metadata and client replay, covering ~25M trades a day and scoring every wallet with ≥ 5 closed trips (order 10^4 a week) with slot-exact pricing at zero marginal credit cost.

### Inferences

**A. Free tier ($0/mo), desktop only**
- Live: public-RPC `logsSubscribe` on the pump curve while the app runs (1.26 GB/h, free); Parsed Streams on followed wallets (1 credit/event); PumpSwap per-pool only for book wallets (as planned). Optionally try publicnode's free Yellowstone gRPC for both programs, but verify PumpSwap coverage first (its WebSocket delivered none).
- Backfill and pricing: Helius Free, 1M credits; ~350 credits per wallet fully follower-scored (30–100 for history, ~320 for 30 priced trips) → ~2,800 wallets/month ≈ 650/week if the whole month goes to scoring, ~300 replays/month at the current 100k allowance. Cache coin pages across wallets to stretch it.
- Metadata: first signature via `signatures` mode (10 credits) + one `getTransaction` (1); no labels.
- Scored per week: ~500–700 fully priced; the live book grows ~1,150 wallets per hour the app is open, with the survivorship bias the repo found (coins the feed missed). Coverage of the market: a few percent of PumpSwap, the curve only while open.

**B. ~$50/mo, two variants**
- B1 Helius Developer ($49): 50 req/s, 10M credits, Enhanced WebSockets, Parsed Streams 10 connections, Wallet API `funded-by`. Spend: ~1M on followed-wallet streams, ~1M on `funded-by` for the top 10k, ~8M on scoring → ~23k wallets/month ≈ 5–6k/week fully chain-priced, plus labels and age. Still desktop-only and still partial market coverage (24/7 curve streaming on Helius metering alone would need ~18M credits, so the live feed stays on public RPC). Fits the existing code paths with no backend.
- B2 Dune Analyst ($75, slightly over): one vendor-hosted weekly SQL over `dex_solana.trades` (block_slot, trader_id, amounts) computes trips, +9-slot follower fills and the 40/min bot rule for every wallet in the week; the app downloads a ranked CSV (tens of thousands of rows) and runs its own Helius replay only on the shortlist. Scores every active wallet (10^4–10^5) per week with ~3 h lag; costs are per query, so adding wallets is free. Risks: curve coverage of the table unverified, API result-size limits and SQL API terms unread, one shared vendor key.
- B3 Solana Tracker Advanced (€50) as a per-wallet history source (200k calls ≈ 100k+ wallet pages) is cheap but its competing-product clause and unknown slot/freshness make it a fallback, not a base.
- Recommended at $50: B1 if the product must stay keyless-backend; B2 if whole-market ranking matters more than live freshness. Both honour "fastest source first": the desktop keeps the chain replay as the verifier.

**C. ~$500/mo, backend plus desktop**
- Stream: Shyft Build $199 (10 connections, unlimited bandwidth, 150k-address filters; or Corvus $160 / SVS $150 / Chainstack ~$98 if unmetered) with one transaction filter on the pump.fun and PumpSwap programs (add Raydium AMM/LaunchLab program ids to the same filter; bandwidth rises). Metered alternatives cost more for the same bytes: Triton ~$580, Alchemy ~$540, QuickNode Scale $499 (fits inside 950M credits), Helius Business ~$720 with overage.
- Indexer: a small VPS (estimate $20–40/mo; not sourced) running the shaurya35/Carbon pattern: Postgres, batch writer with checkpoint, `fromSlot` replay inside the provider window, RPC backfill beyond it (Helius `getTransactionsForAddress` per coin or `getBlock` per slot). ~25M trade rows/day, ~175M/week; score nightly: trips per wallet, slot+9 follower fills from the table itself (zero marginal credits), bot rules, shrinkage ranking, creator links from create instructions.
- Helius Developer $49 for `funded-by` labels, DAS, and the desktop's own replay (the client still prices the shortlist itself, chain first, inside its allowance).
- Total ≈ $199 + $49 + ~$30 ≈ $280–$330; the remainder buys a second region or a Parquet month ($200) for a months-long ranking backtest.
- What it buys: every curve, PumpSwap and Raydium trade; every active wallet (~530k/day touch PumpSwap) scored; the rankable set with ≥ 5 closed trips at human speed is on the order of 10^4 a week (the repo's curve-only rate of 40–157/h extrapolates to 7k–26k/week before PumpSwap). The survivorship bias disappears because the backend sees every trade, not just the ones the desktop was awake for.
- Desktop role: publishes nothing, pulls a compact ranked list (tens of thousands of rows, a few MB), keeps the live follow and replay on the user's own key.

**Cross-cutting**
- Terms: C and B2 move the Scout off pump.fun's services entirely (only usernames/leaderboard remain pump.fun-only); check Helius's "distribute/derivative" wording before a vendor key serves many users; Solana Tracker needs written authorization for a ranking product.
- Nothing below Business on Helius should carry a 24/7 whole-market stream; Helius is the per-wallet and per-coin tool, flat-rate gRPC is the market tool.
